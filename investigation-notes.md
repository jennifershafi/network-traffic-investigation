# Network Traffic Investigation Notes

## Scenario

A workstation was flagged for unusual outbound network activity. A packet capture was reviewed to identify the systems involved, protocols used, destination addresses, and any traffic that may require further investigation.

## Investigation Goals

- Identify the source workstation.
- Review DNS activity.
- Identify external destination IP addresses.
- Examine TCP connections and destination ports.
- Look for unusual outbound communication.
- Determine which activity should be investigated further.

## Tools

- Wireshark
- TCP/IP filtering
- DNS analysis
- Packet inspection

## Analyst Notes

Findings will be recorded after reviewing the packet capture in Wireshark.
